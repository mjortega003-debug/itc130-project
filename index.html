About RetroExchange

Preserving the Legacy

RetroExchange connects passionate gamers and collectors by preserving the legacy of retro gaming. Our goal is to make classic titles accessible through a trusted online platform that blends nostalgia with modern convenience.



Our Story

Founded in 2025 by Team P1, RetroExchange started as a small project to help local collectors swap duplicates. Today, it has grown into a full-featured marketplace for the rarest finds in gaming history.
